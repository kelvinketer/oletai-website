Bridging the Trust Deficit in Agriculture

Millions of smallholder farmers in Africa lack the formal credit history required to access high-quality inputs. Oletai Agro Group replaces traditional collateral with behavioral data and continuous education.

THE CHALLENGE

Financial Exclusion & Poor Yields

Farmers rely on outdated techniques and substandard inputs because they cannot access smart capital. Local Agrovets struggle with manual, risky credit lines (the "book"), limiting local economic growth.

OUR SOLUTION

The Agri-Trust Ecosystem

We digitize agricultural knowledge and financial transactions. By completing localized training modules, farmers build an immutable Trust Score, unlocking digital credit that can only be spent on verified inputs.

How Rahisi Pay Works

A seamless loop from education to harvest.

1

Learn & Scan

Farmers complete hyper-local agronomy modules in our offline-first Academy and use AI to diagnose crop diseases in the field.

2

Earn Smart Capital

Positive behaviors increase the farmer's Agri-Trust Score, automatically unlocking micro-credit lines funded by our capital partners.

3

Redeem Locally

Funds are locked into the Rahisi Pay ecosystem. Farmers use "Scan-to-Pay" QR codes at approved local Agrovets to buy verified seeds and fertilizer.

Digital Cooperatives

Empowering Communities, Not Just Individuals

Technology alone cannot replace the power of local communities. Rahisi Pay digitizes the traditional cooperative model, allowing farmers to form localized groups for shared learning, support, and financial growth.

  • 👩🏽‍🌾
    Women & Youth First Targeted onboarding strategies to ensure marginalized agricultural demographics have direct access to smart capital and agronomy training.
  • 📍
    Hub-and-Spoke Training We empower local Group Leaders who champion digital literacy and climate-smart agriculture within their own villages.
  • 📈
    Group Agri-Trust Scores Communities that learn and repay together unlock higher credit limits and lower rates, driven by positive social accountability.
