Why the Old System Fails

The roots of a financial drought

Africa's agricultural sector employs around half its workforce and holds the potential to become a global breadbasket. Yet this immense opportunity is constrained by a systemic funding gap that has persisted for generations.

Traditional lenders routinely treat agriculture as high-risk — deterred by climate volatility, the remoteness of rural communities, and the fragmented scale of smallholder farming. For the farmers themselves, barriers are just as formidable. Most lack formal land titles to use as collateral. Many operate entirely in cash, with no documented credit history, effectively locking them out of the formal financial system.

The result is a self-reinforcing cycle: low investment leads to low yields, which deepens the perception of risk, which reduces investment further. In Kenya, lenders have demanded collateral of up to 120% of the loan value — leaving most smallholder farmers, and particularly women-led households, with nowhere to turn.

Oletai's Response

How we're plowing through the obstacles

Oletai operates at the intersection of social enterprise and technology innovation — with the mission focus of an NGO and the discipline of a sustainable business. We do not replace the farmer's trust with paperwork. We build it through knowledge.

📚

Replace Collateral with Knowledge

Instead of demanding land titles, we measure what farmers know and how they act. Completing verified agronomy modules builds an Agri-Trust Score that unlocks credit — a model research institutions identify as critical to closing the access gap.

🔗

Strengthen the Entire Value Chain

Rahisi Agro Pay connects farmers, Agrovets, and input suppliers in a single closed-loop digital ecosystem — ensuring capital flows to verified inputs, not lost in cash leakage. This value chain financing approach secures the entire agricultural network from seed to harvest.

🌍

Turn Data into De-risking

The AI biosurveillance network generates real-time agronomic data that makes farmers legible to financial partners — addressing the critical "lack of reliable data" barrier that has long excluded smallholder farmers from formal credit markets.

♀️

Prioritise Women & Youth

Women-led farms face 20–30% lower access to finance than men. Our targeted onboarding is designed to close this gap directly — prioritising intentional inclusivity in agricultural financing from the ground up.

How It Works

A Seamless Loop from Education to Harvest

1

Learn & Scan

Farmers complete hyper-local agronomy modules in the offline-first academy and use AI to photograph and instantly diagnose crop diseases in their field — contributing anonymised data to the national biosurveillance network.

2

Earn Smart Capital

Positive behaviours — completing modules, repaying loans, reporting field data — increase the farmer's Agri-Trust Score. Higher scores automatically unlock larger micro-credit lines funded by our institutional capital partners.

3

Redeem Locally

Funds are locked within the Rahisi Pay ecosystem. Farmers use QR-code vouchers at approved local Agrovets to purchase verified seeds, fertilisers, and agrochemicals — ensuring every shilling is spent on productive inputs.

Digital Cooperatives

Empowering Communities, Not Just Individuals

Technology alone cannot replace the power of local communities. Rahisi Pay digitises the traditional cooperative model, enabling farmers to form localised groups for shared learning, peer support, and collective financial growth.

Translational Research

Citizen Science Meets Academic Rigour

Every AI scan uploaded by a Rahisi Pay farmer feeds a decentralised biosurveillance network — providing unparalleled, real-time data on disease vectors, pest outbreaks, and yield anomalies across Kenya.

🌍

Citizen Science Network

Farmers act as frontline biosurveillance agents. Each crop photo generates anonymised geospatial data that ICIPE, JKUAT, and KALRO researchers access for early outbreak detection.

📊

Live Pest & Disease Heatmaps

Real-time geographic tracking of Fall Armyworm, Maize Lethal Necrosis, coffee rust, and other critical threats — enabling rapid early-warning alerts to affected farming communities.

🔬

Research-to-Classroom Pipeline

Complex peer-reviewed findings from JKUAT and KALRO are digitised into 5-minute interactive micro-modules — making the latest agricultural science accessible to every farmer with a smartphone.
